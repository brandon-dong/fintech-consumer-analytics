Descriptive Analytics  ·  What Happened?
Checking and Savings Account Leads All Fintech Products
at 212,760 Complaints — Peaking at 70,316 in January 2025
Checking and savings account complaints outpace every other fintech product category — reflecting the breadth of everyday banking touchpoints where consumers escalate to the CFPB.
90% of all complaints are filed via the CFPB's web portal, confirming that fintech's digitally native customer base is equally comfortable escalating online.
The January 2025 peak of 70,316 complaints in a single month signals post-holiday account management stress — disputes over charges, access issues, and account closures spike each January.

Diagnostic Analytics  ·  Why Did It Happen?
Greenlight Is 15× More Likely to Miss CFPB's Response Window —
Account Management Failures Drive 37% of Its Complaints
The fintech sector floor (money transfer products) misses CFPB's 15-day response window just 1.4% of the time. Greenlight misses it 21.1% of the time — a 15× gap versus the best-in-class benchmark.
"Managing an account" is Greenlight's #1 complaint issue, representing 7 of its 19 CFPB complaints (37%) — signaling gaps in account support workflows, not product defects.
Account closures, fraud/scam resolution, and onboarding problems round out Greenlight's complaint profile — all categories where faster triage and routing would directly reduce escalation.
▲  21.1% late-response rate — 15× above the sector floor

Recommendation
Fix Account Routing First — Greenlight Can Close a 15× Response Gap Without Changing Its Product
Action
Automate account management complaint triage with a 72-hour initial response SLA — targeting the "Managing an account" and "Closing an account" issue types that drive 47% of Greenlight's CFPB escalations.
→
Expected Outcome
Bring Greenlight's late-response rate from 21.1% to the sector minimum of ≤1.4% — a 15× improvement that directly reduces CFPB regulatory exposure for a product serving 6.5M families.
Why this works: The diagnostic data shows response timing — not product quality — is Greenlight's primary CFPB escalation driver. Automated routing addresses the root cause without requiring feature changes or pricing restructuring.
Why it matters for Greenlight: With 6.5M users, each percentage-point reduction in late responses represents thousands fewer CFPB-escalated cases, lower regulatory risk, and stronger consumer trust signals for a company whose brand promise is family financial safety.
The sector benchmark exists: Money transfer products hit 98.6% timely response at scale. That's not aspirational — it's the floor. Greenlight has a clear, proven target to close toward.
