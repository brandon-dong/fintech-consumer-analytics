## Overview

If you're considering getting a prepaid card or account, the CFPB has information that can help you choose the right one and better understand your rights.

## Shopping for Prepaid Cards

The CFPB's prepaid rule requires fee disclosures to help consumers shop. Key guidance:
- Not all prepaid cards are the same — each has its own terms and fees
- Register your card to get important protections
- Most prepaid cards charge fees: maintenance, purchase, withdrawal

## Common Fee Types
- Additional card fee
- ATM balance inquiry fee
- Bill payment fee
- Card cancellation fee
- Card replacement fee
- Card-to-card transfer fee
- Cash reload fee
- Decline fee
- Foreign transaction fee

## Consumer Rights
- Consumers can get money back if a card or funds are lost or stolen
- Employers cannot force employees to use a payroll card for wages
- Government benefit cards (EBT, Social Security) have specific protections

## Complaint Submission
Having trouble with a financial product? If you've already tried reaching out to the company and still have an issue, you can submit a complaint — CFPB will forward it to the company and work to get a response, generally within 15 days.

## Relevance to Consumer Analytics

Prepaid cards (including kids' debit cards like Greenlight) fall under the "Credit card or prepaid card" product category in the CFPB complaint database. Common prepaid card complaint issues include:
- Unexpected fees
- Card replacement problems
- Transaction dispute resolution failures
- Account access after card loss
