## Greenlight Infinity Plan — Safety Features

The Infinity plan is Greenlight's premium tier, combining family banking with physical safety features.

## Getting Started with Infinity
- Earn 5% on savings (promotional rate)
- Family safety features integrated into one app
- Crash detection on family devices
- Location sharing for all family members

## Driving Safety Features
- Real-time trip alerts with driving scores
- Unsafe action tracking per trip
- Crash detection alerts
- Driving snapshot email reports

## Family Location Sharing
- See family member locations within the app
- Place alerts for specific locations (e.g., "Fiona arrived at school")
- Parents can see kids' locations; kids choose what to share with siblings
- Location data not sold to third parties

## SOS Alerts
- Kids can send SOS alerts to parents
- Data shared with 911 only in emergencies
- Auto insurance companies cannot access driving data

## Permissions & Privacy
- Greenlight does not sell personal information collected via safety features
- Data shared with 911 in crash/SOS situations only
- Auto insurance companies cannot access driving data or crash notifications

## Relevance to Consumer Complaints

Safety and privacy features are a common source of consumer complaints in family fintech — issues like:
- Unexpected location tracking
- Data retention after account closure
- Disputes over crash detection false positives
- Account access issues after a family member leaves the plan
