## Overview

Based on Greenlight's own user data, average weekly allowance climbs with age. Published Feb 6, 2025.

Key stats:
- In 2024, average weekly allowance for Greenlight kids ages 5–19: **$12.98**
- Nationally, $19.39 is the mean weekly allowance (T. Rowe Price)
- ~80% of U.S. parents give kids an allowance; 64% require kids to earn it through chores

## Year-over-Year Trend (2023 vs 2024)

Average allowances declined from 2023 to 2024 across most ages:
- 13-year-olds: $13.01 (2023) → $11.57 (2024)
- 17-year-olds: $23.69 (2023) → $20.87 (2024)

## Relevance to Consumer Analytics

Greenlight's proprietary data on allowance flows indicates the average transaction volume per family account. Knowing that a typical 10-year-old processes ~$8.44/week helps contextualize dispute and complaint severity: a $50 disputed transaction represents 6 weeks of allowance for a 10-year-old — materially significant to the family. This frame helps explain elevated consumer_disputed_flag rates in children's debit/prepaid card products.
