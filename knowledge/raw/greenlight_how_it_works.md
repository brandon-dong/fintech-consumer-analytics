## Greenlight: Smart Money Tools and Built-In Safety

Greenlight is a family financial app with debit cards for kids, teens, and seniors. 6.5 million parents and kids use Greenlight.

## Product Overview

- Sign up and choose a plan for your family
- Download the Greenlight app
- Order debit cards for kids and/or seniors
- Every family member gets their own login — with a secure app experience built for them
- Parents manage. Kids learn. Seniors stay safe.
- Plans start at $5.99/month for the whole family (up to 5 kids)

## Key Features

### Customize Settings
- Assign chores and automate allowance
- Control how much (and where) kids spend
- Set savings goals and watch them grow

### Safety Features
- Turn on safety features tailored to each family member
- Customize place alerts for their go-to locations
- Link external accounts to monitor for suspicious activity

## Pricing

Plans start at just $5.99/month for the whole family, including up to five kids.

## Market Position

Greenlight targets families as a financial literacy and safety product — positioned as more than a debit card, it's a platform for teaching money management to children and teens while giving parents control and visibility.

## Relevance to Consumer Analytics

As a fintech company serving children/families, Greenlight operates in the "Checking or savings account" and "Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service" product categories tracked by the CFPB.
