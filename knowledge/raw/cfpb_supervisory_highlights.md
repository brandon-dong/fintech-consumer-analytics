## Supervisory Highlights

The CFPB's Supervisory Highlights reports share key findings from supervisory activities. These reports help industry limit risks to consumers and comply with federal consumer financial laws.

### Program Overview

CFPB supervision reviews institutions for compliance with federal consumer financial laws. Supervisory actions have required changes to compliance management systems to prevent violations and reduce risks to consumers.

### Key Supervision Areas (from historic reports)

- **Compliance management systems** — ensuring companies have adequate internal controls
- **Mortgage servicing** — monitoring for unfair, deceptive, or abusive acts or practices (UDAAP)
- **Fair lending** — ensuring equitable access to credit
- **Fintech and digital financial services** — emerging area of supervisory focus

### Significance for Consumer Analytics

Supervisory Highlights reveal systemic patterns in how financial companies treat consumers — including issues like wrongful fees, inaccurate credit reporting, and discriminatory practices. These patterns are visible in the complaint database as spikes in specific issue types.

The CFPB publishes these reports to create accountability and help companies understand what behaviors draw regulatory scrutiny.
