## Overview

CFPB's financial education program for families, helping children build money skills and habits through age-appropriate activities.

## Age-Based Financial Milestones

CFPB tracks whether children are reaching age-appropriate money milestones in three tiers:
- **Young children** — basic concepts (saving, wants vs. needs)
- **School-age children to preteens** — budgeting, earning, comparison shopping
- **Teens to young adults** — credit, banking, investing, debt management

## Financial Literacy Topics (for parent conversations)

- Buying a car
- Getting a pet
- Moving to a new house
- Paying bills
- Paying with a credit card

## Research Foundation

Based on CFPB research into how children develop financial capability:
- CFPB studied "the way children develop the abilities and attributes that contribute to their financial well-being in adulthood"
- Identified developmental milestones for financial capability
- Framework connects activities to children's developmental stages

## Program Origin

Recommended by the President's Advisory Council on Financial Capability. Built on 1+ year of research drawing from dozens of standards, curricula, and academic studies.

## Connection to Greenlight

Both CFPB's "Money as You Grow" and Greenlight target the same population — families teaching children financial literacy. The CFPB provides the regulatory framework and educational standards; Greenlight provides the commercial product. When Greenlight products generate complaints, they represent failures in delivering on this shared financial literacy promise.
