## The CFPB

We aim to make consumer financial markets work for consumers, responsible providers, and the economy as a whole. We protect consumers from unfair, deceptive, or abusive practices and take action against companies that break the law.

## Core Functions

- Rooting out unfair, deceptive, or abusive acts or practices by writing rules, supervising companies, and enforcing the law
- Enforcing laws that outlaw discrimination in consumer finance
- Taking consumer complaints
- Enhancing financial education
- Researching the consumer experience of using financial products
- Monitoring financial markets for new risks to consumers

## Fast Facts: CFPB by the Numbers (as of December 3, 2024)

- **$21 billion+**: Amount of monetary compensation, principal reductions, canceled debts, and other consumer relief resulting from CFPB enforcement ($19 billion) and supervisory ($1.7 billion) work.
- **205 million+**: Estimated number of consumers or consumer accounts eligible to receive relief from CFPB's enforcement and supervisory work.
- **$5 billion+**: Civil money penalties imposed by the CFPB on companies and individuals that violate the law.
- **$6.1 billion**: Estimated amount consumers will save every year due to recent changes in banks' overdraft and NSF fee policies.
- **22.8 million**: Number of people expected to have had at least one medical collection removed from their credit reports.
- **6.8 million+**: Consumer complaints sent to companies for response — including **4.6 million+** about credit reporting, **83,000+** about medical debt collection, and **96,000+** about student loans.
- **63 million+**: Users who have accessed the CFPB's "Ask CFPB" financial questions database.
- **35**: Number of Supervisory Highlights issued documenting key examination findings.
- **8**: Languages CFPB provides translated consumer-facing materials in.

## Relevance to Consumer Analytics

These statistics frame the scale of the complaint ecosystem. With 6.8M+ complaints sent to companies, even a company receiving 0.1% of complaints represents tens of thousands of consumer interactions tracked by the CFPB. The $6.1B in fee savings also signals active regulatory pressure on banking products — a key driver of complaint patterns in checking/savings accounts.
