## Overview

Published: July 29, 2025. Guide for parents on protecting children's online safety and digital security.

## Common Online Dangers for Kids and Teens

- Cyberbullying from peers or strangers
- Inappropriate or explicit content
- Online predators pretending to be friends
- Phishing scams or trick messages
- Oversharing on social media
- Malware or spyware from downloads or pop-ups
- Harmful online challenges or trends
- **Pressure to spend money in games or apps** — directly relevant to consumer complaints

## 7 Ways to Help Keep Kids Safe Online

1. **Go online together** — supervised internet use, especially for young children
2. **Keep devices in shared spaces** — normalize visibility without surveillance feeling
3. **Use parental controls and filters** — built into iOS, Android, ISPs, app stores, and social platforms
4. **Teach kids what's safe to share** — full names, phone numbers, addresses, passwords, photos are sensitive
5. **Encourage speaking up** — create safety to report concerns without punishment
6. **Help kids recognize red flags** — unknown links, strange messages, gift offers, secrecy requests
7. **Protect devices and computers** — OS updates, antivirus software, security patches
