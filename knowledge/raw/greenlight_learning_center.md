## Relevance to Consumer Analytics

The Learning Center positions Greenlight as a financial education platform, not just a payment product. This framing is important for understanding the company's customer promise — and the gap when complaints arise. A company that markets financial literacy but generates complaints about account access or unauthorized transactions faces heightened reputational risk, which is visible in the `consumer_disputed_flag` rate in our mart data.
