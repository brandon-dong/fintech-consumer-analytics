### Money Transfer / Fintech Enforcement Pattern
The Wise enforcement action (Jan 30, 2025 and May 15, 2025 amendment) is particularly relevant: Wise is in the same CFPB product category ("Money transfer, virtual currency, or money service") as several fintech products. The action shows regulators actively monitoring fee transparency and disclosure in digital transfer products.

## Regulatory Environment Context

The CFPB is undergoing significant leadership and priority changes under the Trump administration (2025). Key shifts:
- Reduced enforcement prioritization in some areas
- "Humility Pledge" signals less aggressive supervision
- Some cases being withdrawn
- Focus remaining on servicemembers, veterans, and pressing consumer threats

## Implication for Complaint Data Analysis

CFPB enforcement de-prioritization may reduce complaint follow-up in some categories, but complaint filing by consumers continues independently. Our complaint database captures consumer-initiated complaints regardless of CFPB enforcement posture, making the data a lagging but robust indicator of consumer harm even in periods of regulatory pullback.
